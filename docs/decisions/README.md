# Decisions

Why the shop is the way it is. The [decision log](decision-log.md) records what the owner decided and what was decided while building, each with its reason and its date. The feature documents say what the shop does today; this folder says why.

## How the log is kept

- One section per round of decisions, headed by its topic and its date. A new section goes at the top of the file, under the introduction.
- A section is a table: the decision, and the reason. The owner's own words are quoted where they set a rule.
- A section is not rewritten when the shop changes later: a later decision gets its own section and says what it replaces. The log is a record, so it names files and paths as they were on its day ([old paths](../archive/README.md#old-paths)).
- What was deliberately not built, and what the API could not do, is written down too: it answers the question before it is asked again.
