This shop uses only the cookies and browser storage it needs to work. There is no tracking, no advertising and no visit statistics. That is also why we do not ask for consent: none is needed for storage that is technically necessary.

## What is stored

| Name                  | Kind            | Purpose                                                | Duration                                      |
| --------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| `rf_customer_access`  | Cookie          | Keeps you signed in                                    | 15 minutes                                    |
| `rf_customer_refresh` | Cookie          | Renews the sign-in without asking you to sign in again | 30 days, extended when used, at most 180 days |
| `NEXT_LOCALE`         | Cookie          | Remembers the language you chose                       | Until you close the browser                   |
| `rf.cart.v1`          | Local storage   | Your cart while you are not signed in                  | Until you order or empty it                   |
| `rf.session.v1`       | Local storage   | Remembers that you were signed in with this browser    | Until you sign out                            |
| `rf.checkout.key.v1`  | Session storage | Prevents an order from being placed twice              | Until you close the tab                       |
| `rf.cookie-notice.v1` | Local storage   | Remembers that you have read the cookie notice         | Until you clear the storage                   |

The two sign-in cookies are only set when you sign in.

## Services of other providers

- **Payment:** when you pay online you move to the payment page of Stripe. The cookies and the privacy notice of Stripe apply there.
- **Sign-in with Google:** only if you choose "Continue with Google" on the sign-in page, a script of Google is loaded, which can set cookies of its own.

## Clearing the storage

You can delete cookies and storage at any time in the settings of your browser. Afterwards you are signed out, and the cart of a visit without sign-in is empty.

How we process personal data is described in our privacy policy.
